Privacy

Fontlore collects nothing. This page states exactly what runs, what is stored, and who to contact.

What this site does not do

  • No cookies, no local-storage identifiers, no session storage.
  • No analytics, no telemetry, no beacons, no fingerprinting.
  • No accounts, no forms, no comment systems.
  • No external requests: styles, scripts, and data are served from this site only.

What is processed

Standard server access logs are kept by GitHub Pages (the hosting infrastructure), governed by GitHub's Privacy Statement. This project does not control those logs and does not receive individual visitor data from GitHub.

Content licensing and attribution

Font records link to each typeface project's own source page. Licenses are recorded as SPDX-style identifiers and must be verified at the source before commercial use. The typefaces referenced remain the property of their respective authors and foundries.

Contact

Privacy questions or corrections: open an issue at github.com/izynu/fontlore/issues.
