## Contributing

Individual contributions are tracked in the GitHub contributor graph: see the contributors page of this repository for the full, live list. That page is the source of truth; this file only records project roles, so it never drifts out of sync with actual commits.

First-time contributors start with CONTRIBUTING.md: the validator requirement, the ASCII house rules, and the evidence standard are all there. Good first issues are labeled in the issue tracker, and data corrections with evidence URLs are the most common entry point.

## Roles

- Contributor: anyone with a merged pull request. There are no tiers; a one-line data correction with a good evidence URL ranks alongside a new reference section.
- Reviewer: contributors whose corrections have consistently survived validation may be asked to review data-correction PRs in their area.
- Maintainer: merge rights, release duties, and enforcement duties under CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md.

## Becoming a maintainer

Maintenance is offered, not applied for, when sustainably useful: a history of evidence-based contributions, validator-clean PRs, and calm review behaviour. Maintainers are added by unanimous decision of the existing maintainers, and anyone may decline without explanation.

## Emeritus and stepping back

Maintainers who step back move to emeritus with their history intact. The contributor graph records what they did; nothing is deleted or rewritten on departure.

## Recognition norms

- Data corrections credit the evidence provider in the commit message.
- New reference sections or substantial rewrites credit the author in the commit and, where the contribution is structural, in the file's revision history.
- No contribution counters, leaderboards, or inflated claims appear in this repository; the contributor graph is honest by construction.

## Project history

- Fontlore was rebuilt in the open after an environment loss; the validator-enforced house rules and the honest-counts discipline come from that recovery.
- v1.0 assembled the 165-font data layer, the 9 reference categories, the decision engine, the validator, and the site's tabbed browsing from one shared source of truth.
- The governance files you are reading were written to make the next contributor's defaults explicit: evidence over assertion, nulls over guesses, and review over vibes.
