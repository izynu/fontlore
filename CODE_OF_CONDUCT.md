# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Everyone contributing to Fontlore - issues, discussions, pull requests, reviews - is expected to behave professionally and in good faith. This project's product is trustworthy typography knowledge, and the same honesty applies to how people are treated as to how data is recorded.

## Expected behaviour

- Be accurate. Correct others with evidence and accept correction the same way; the null policy applies to claims about people and projects too.
- Be constructive in review. Critique the data, the claim, or the code, never the person.
- Assume competence. Questions are welcome; nobody is born knowing font licensing.
- Stay on topic in project spaces, and keep disagreements about typography technical.
- Give credit. Name the evidence, the foundry, and the contributor whose work you built on.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or trolling in any project space.
- Deliberate disruption: bad-faith issues, spam, or repeatedly reopening settled decisions.
- Publishing other people's private information without consent.
- Sexualized language or imagery in any project space.
- Misrepresenting the project: fake endorsements, fabricated metrics, or claiming maintainer approval that does not exist.

This covers the repository, its issues and discussions, the CI and release channels, and any space where you are acting as a representative of the project.

## Scope and enforcement

Maintainers enforce this standard. Behaviour that violates it will be addressed with escalating responses depending on severity: a private warning, a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces, or removal of contributions. Edits and decisions made in the course of normal technical disagreement are not enforcement matters; argue the typography, not the typographer. Maintainers are held to the same standard and do not adjudicate reports about themselves; the other maintainer or, if both are implicated, the GitHub staff escalation path handles those.

## Reporting

To report a problem:

- Open a GitHub issue in this repository and mark it for maintainer attention, or
- If the matter is sensitive or involves a maintainer, contact Zynu (@izynu) or Shraddha (@xhraddha) directly via the email or contact channel listed on their GitHub profiles.

Reports will be acknowledged within a few days and handled confidentially as far as the platform allows. We will tell you what action was taken. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is itself a violation.
